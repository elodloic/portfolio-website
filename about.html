How I became a web developer 💻

I’m a tech-savvy professional with over five years of experience across SaaS, IT support, and logistics. I began my career in technical support, where I developed strong problem-solving and communication skills while supporting financial software and troubleshooting complex IT issues. In recent years, I transitioned to SaaS start-ups, specializing in product optimization, customer success and AI chatbot implementation. My ability to combine technical knowledge with user-centric communication has helped me streamline workflows and improve customer experiences. Currently, I’m enhancing my skill set with a Full Stack Web Development program, gaining expertise in modern web technologies, database management and software testing. With a passion for innovation and simplicity, I’m eager to contribute to projects that merge technology with intuitive solutions. Whether it’s coding, documentation or technical collaboration, my goal is to bridge technical needs and user success.

How I got into gravel cycling 🚵

What started as a daily commute quickly became a passion. One day, I discovered an off-road shortcut that shaved five minutes off my route—and I was hooked! Since then, I’ve explored countless gravel trails, enjoying the blend of adventure, nature, and fitness. Cycling has taught me the value of persistence and always seeking the path less traveled—lessons I carry into my work as well.
